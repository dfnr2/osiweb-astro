title: Books
description: Collection of books and publications about OSI computers and systems
---

| Book | Description |
|------|-------------|
| [Micro on the OSI](/books/Micro_on_the_OSI.pdf) | Compilation of Micro articles for OSI |
| [First Book of OSI](/books/first_book_of_OSI.pdf) | Elcomp - First Book of OSI, scanned and contributed by Stephan (stm). Lots of articles on OSI machines, compiled from various sources. |
| [Second Book of OSI index](/books/Second_Book_of_OSI_Contents.pdf) | Contents of the second book of OSI. Scanned and contributed by Mike. |
| [Third Book of OSI](/books/Third_Book_of_OSI.pdf) | This is based on an expansion system for 6502 micros. Articles describe the ELCOMP expansion bus, and projects based on the bus: 6522/6520 I/O, EPROM programmer, Sound card, memory cards, A/D converter. It seems useful, but not much OSI stuff that can stand on its own. |
| [Fourth Book of OSI index](/books/Fourth_Book_of_OSI_contents.pdf) | Contents of the fourth book of OSI, Scanned and contributed by Mike. |
| [Fifth Book of OSI](/books/Fifth_Book_of_OSI.pdf) | This is a collection of articles, programs, and tips for the OSI C1/C2/C4 machines. Many thanks to Steve Gray for the scan!! |
| [Aardvark's First Book of OSI](/books/aardvark-first-book-of-osi.pdf) | Aardvark's first book of OSI, lots of misc. info on OSI systems. Scanned and contributed by Bill Dromgoole. |
| [OSI BASIC in ROM](/books/OSI_Basic_In_Rom.PDF) | An introduction to OSI (Microsoft 6502) BASIC in ROM, along with in-depth discussion of variable and program storage and other under-the-hood details. Contributed by Jeff Ferguson. |
